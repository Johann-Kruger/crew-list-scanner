Install on this iPhone: tap the Share button (square with arrow), then Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app and works with no signal.

The list is kept only on this phone until you clear it. Photos are read on this phone and are not stored or sent anywhere.

For a good read

  • Passport: photograph the photo page. The two lines of letters and <<< at the bottom must be sharp and fill most of the width.
  • Smart ID card: photograph the back, with the big barcode sharp. That gives the best result. The front also works if the back won't read.
  • Green ID book: photograph the page with the photo and barcode. Keep the barcode sharp and the whole page in the frame.
  • Avoid glare from lights and windows. Lay the document flat, hold the phone straight above it.
